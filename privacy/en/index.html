1. The short version

"Together" is a quit-smoking tracker. It works without registration and without an account: we do not know your name, phone number, e-mail address or precise location.

The app does use the internet. Some information is sent to our servers and to our providers' servers:

  • an anonymous device identifier (a random UUID generated on your phone);
  • technical usage statistics (launches, time in the app, active days);
  • your nickname and messages — only if you use the public chat;
  • data required to display ads (through Yandex's advertising SDK).

Your entries, settings, quit progress, the date and time of your last cigarette, notes and triggers are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.

If you never use the chat, we receive nothing that could identify you as a person.

6. Security

  • Data between the app and Firebase/Yandex servers is transmitted over encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Data on Firebase servers is protected by Google's safeguards (encryption at rest, access control).
  • Local data is protected by the Android app sandbox and is not accessible to other apps.
  • Only the developer and authorised persons have access to administrative data.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We recommend not entering information you consider especially sensitive.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  1. Know what data about you is processed and obtain a copy.
  2. Request rectification, restriction or erasure of data that is incomplete, inaccurate or unlawfully processed.
  3. Withdraw consent — simply stop using the relevant features and uninstall the app.
  4. Object to processing for analytics purposes.
  5. Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — your national data protection authority, or Roskomnadzor if you are in Russia.

How to delete your data

Local data: "Android Settings → Apps → Together → Storage → Clear data", or uninstall the app. This deletes your progress, notes, settings, in-game currency and UUID with no way to restore them.

Cloud data: e-mail newkostenko1@yandex.ru with the subject "Data deletion". Include your chat nickname (if you have one), the approximate install date and the store you installed the app from — we will use these to locate your record. If you know your device identifier, include it: it speeds up the search. We will delete your statistics, nickname, messages, gifts and bans within 30 calendar days, unless we have a legal obligation to keep them longer. If the information is not sufficient to identify you unambiguously, we will ask for clarification and delete everything we can reliably match to your request.

Note that uninstalling the app does not automatically delete chat messages and statistics already sent — that requires a request to newkostenko1@yandex.ru.

8. Children

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over: it concerns quitting smoking and contains game mechanics and advertising. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, e-mail newkostenko1@yandex.ru and we will delete it.

9. Medical disclaimer

The app is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or prescribe treatment. All figures (money saved, avoided nicotine and tar, body recovery) are calculated from averaged scientific and medical data and do not account for your individual circumstances. They are informational only and are not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

If you need help quitting smoking, consult a doctor or a specialist service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
